Privacy Policy for Pipe-Dash-Run

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how the game "Pipe-Dash-Run" (the "Game"), published by DG-Tech, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The Game does not require an account, and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your best score, coin balance, unlocked skins, and sound preference locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you clear the app's or browser's storage, or uninstall the Game.

Third-party services

The Game does not currently include advertising, analytics, or other third-party tracking. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and will name the services used.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
